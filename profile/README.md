# Madrid School of Business and Technology

An international graduate school for business and technology, based in Madrid.

We offer 12-month online master's programmes, taught in English and designed for the age of artificial intelligence. Our programmes are built on three principles: learning to use AI with judgement, developing the human skills that set professionals apart, and preparing for the roles of the future.

## Our programmes

- [Master of Business Administration (MBA)](https://madridbusinesstech.com/mba.html)
- [Master in Applied Artificial Intelligence](https://madridbusinesstech.com/ai.html)
- [Master in Data Protection](https://madridbusinesstech.com/data-protection.html)
- [Master in Marketing and Digital Strategy](https://madridbusinesstech.com/marketing.html)

Programmes available from September 2027.
